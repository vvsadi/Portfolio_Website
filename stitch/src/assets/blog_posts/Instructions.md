This is **sample content** that follows the same shape as `_template.md` in this folder. When your blog is connected to these Markdown files, this paragraph will appear as the opening of your article.

## Why a template helps

You only need to repeat a few steps for each new idea you want to publish:

1. Duplicate `_template.md` (or an older post) and give the copy a **new filename**.
2. Fill in the **settings block** at the top: title, date, unique **slug**, and a short **excerpt** (blurb).
3. Delete the placeholder text under the second `---` and write your post.

## What you can use in the body

Here are common building blocks:

- **Bullets** for short lists.
- *Italics* or **bold** for emphasis.
- A [link to another site](https://developer.mozilla.org/) when you want to cite a resource.

> A blockquote is good for a quote or a callout you want to stand apart from normal paragraphs.

```js
// Code snippets can live in a fenced block

  return `Hello, ${name}!`;
```
