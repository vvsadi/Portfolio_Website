Most people think that building something is the hard part.

It’s not.

The hard part is starting when you are unsure, continuing when you face criticism, and strongly believing it matters when efforts don’t yield any result immediately.

We try to seek validation before we begin – “Will it work?”, “Can I do this?” – questions that can make you rethink your decision. 

Well, you will never know if you haven’t tried it. And to try, you need to start somewhere.

For instance, I started this blog without knowing where it would go, simply because I wanted to build something new.

That’s the point. You don’t know. You start anyway.
